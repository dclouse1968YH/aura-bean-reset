Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: December 10, 2024

1. Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Display name (optional, chosen by you)
  • User ID (automatically generated)

Wellness Data You Create:

  • Mood logs and emotional check-ins
  • Journal entries and reflections
  • Meditation and breathing practice sessions
  • Weather and sleep quality inputs

Health Data (Optional):

  • Sleep data from Apple HealthKit (only if you grant permission)
  • We only read sleep data; we never write to HealthKit
  • You can revoke this permission anytime in your device Settings

Automatically Collected:

  • Device type and operating system version (for compatibility)
  • App usage analytics (anonymous, for improving features)
  • Crash reports (to fix bugs and improve stability)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and improve AuraBean's services:

  • App Functionality: To display your mood trends, journal entries, and wellbeing insights
  • Personalization: To greet you by name and tailor your experience
  • Sleep Insights: To correlate your mood with sleep patterns (if you enable HealthKit)
  • Account Management: To authenticate you and sync your data across devices
  • Service Improvement: To understand how features are used and fix issues

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to anyone
  • Use your data for advertising or marketing
  • Share your wellness data with third parties
  • Track you across other apps or websites

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS
  • Database Security: Your data is stored in Supabase with row-level security policies that ensure only you can access your own data
  • Authentication: We use secure authentication methods including Sign in with Apple
  • Access Controls: Only you can view your mood logs, journal entries, and personal data

4. Third-Party Services

AuraBean uses the following trusted third-party services:

Supabase (Database & Authentication):

  • Stores your account information and app data
  • Provides secure authentication
  • Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

RevenueCat (In-App Purchases):

  • Manages subscriptions and premium features
  • Processes payments through Apple
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple HealthKit (Optional):

  • Only accessed if you grant permission
  • Sleep data stays on your device unless you choose to sync it
  • Governed by Apple's privacy policies

5. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • When you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days
  • Deleted data cannot be recovered

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data we have about you through the app
  • Update: Edit your display name, email, and other profile information
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings
  • Export: Request a copy of your data (contact us at support@aurabean.me)
  • Revoke: Disable HealthKit access anytime through iOS Settings

7. Children's Privacy

AuraBean is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect
  • Right to delete your personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (note: we do not sell your information)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

9. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right of access to your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last updated" date
  • Notifying you through the app if changes are significant

Your continued use of AuraBean after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in data centers located in different countries. We ensure that all data transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and use appropriate safeguards.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

Email: support@aurabean.me

For privacy-related requests (data access, deletion, export), please include:

  • Your account email address
  • Detailed description of your request
  • We will respond within 30 days
